Our Story

Founded in 2020 by camping enthusiast Rajaram Jadhav, we started with just 5 tents in the Sahyadri mountains. Today, we've grown into Maharashtra's most loved camping destination, hosting 5000+ happy campers every year.

What began as weekend getaways with friends has transformed into a full-fledged camping paradise, preserving nature while creating unforgettable memories.

Our Mission & Vision

Mission

Connect people with nature through sustainable camping experiences that rejuvenate the body, mind, and soul.
